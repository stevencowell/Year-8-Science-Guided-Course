Course map

Outcome codes and taught-content descriptors show the accepted curriculum mapping. They are not quiz content.

Y8S-M01 · Term 1 Weeks 1-4

Data science 1 - part A

  • SC4-DA1-01 — explains how data is used by scientists to model and predict scientific phenomena
  • SC4-WS-06 — uses data to identify trends, patterns and relationships, and draw conclusions
  • SC4-WS-07 — identifies problem-solving strategies and proposes solutions

Taught content: Where scientific data comes from; Digital traces and privacy; Provenance and safe use; Building useful scientific questions; Variables, data types and dataset structure; Collecting data you can trust; Representing and comparing data; Patterns, outliers and conclusions; Evaluating and justifying solutions.

Y8S-M02 · Term 1 Week 6 to Term 2 Week 5

Living systems

  • SC4-LIV-01 — describes the role, structure and function of a range of living systems and their components
  • SC4-WS-02 — identifies questions and makes predictions to guide scientific investigations
  • SC4-WS-05 — uses a variety of ways to process and represent data
  • SC4-WS-08 — communicates scientific concepts and ideas using a range of communication forms

Taught content: Levels of organisation; Major body systems; How body systems work together; Plant organs working together; Specialised cells and transport tissues; A connected plant system; Relationships in ecosystems; Food webs and energy flow; Populations, change and extinction.

Y8S-M03 · Term 2 Week 6 to Term 3 Week 5

Periodic table and atomic structure

  • SC4-PRT-01 — explains how uses of elements and compounds are influenced by scientific understanding and discoveries relating to their properties
  • SC4-WS-05 — uses a variety of ways to process and represent data
  • SC4-WS-06 — uses data to identify trends, patterns and relationships, and draw conclusions

Taught content: Classifying elements, compounds and mixtures; Physical properties and broad material groups; Using evidence to select a material; Inside an atom; Reading simple atomic models; Why scientific models change; Reading element symbols and atomic numbers; Periods, groups and simple electron patterns; From element symbols to chemical formulas.

Y8S-M04 · Term 3 Week 6 to Term 4 Week 5

Change

  • SC4-CHG-01 — explains how energy causes geological and chemical change
  • SC4-WS-01 — uses scientific tools and instruments for observations
  • SC4-WS-03 — plans safe and valid investigations
  • SC4-WS-04 — follows a planned procedure to undertake safe and valid investigations

Taught content: Systems and energy stores; Energy transfers and transformations; Useful energy and energy spread; Physical and chemical changes; Using observations and word equations; Conservation and judging evidence; Geological change and the rock cycle; Fossils, rock layers and relative age; Evidence for moving plates.

Y8S-M05 · Term 4 Weeks 6-10

Data science 1 - part B and depth study

  • SC4-DA1-01 — explains how data is used by scientists to model and predict scientific phenomena
  • SC4-WS-06 — uses data to identify trends, patterns and relationships, and draw conclusions
  • SC4-WS-07 — identifies problem-solving strategies and proposes solutions

Taught content: What scientific models represent; Comparing models with evidence; Limitations and appropriate use; How simulations represent systems; Predictions depend on evidence and assumptions; Sensitivity, limitations and appropriate applications; Building a focused evidence pathway; From evidence to analysis and conclusion; Reflection, improvement and source records.
