How the course works

A steady routine through the year

Use the same four-step learning rhythm across each connected Science module.

1. Learn

Read three focused theory sections and inspect the linked visual or evidence source.

2. Check

Complete ten knowledge checks for each named section and return to the exact theory heading when needed.

3. Explain

Use the written response prompts to explain your reasoning in your own words.

4. Review and keep

Review the feedback and keep the useful written learning in the way your teacher directs.
